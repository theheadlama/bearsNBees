Dev Log

11/28/2025

On Tuesday I decided to throw together an app using the flocking system on p5.js that I'd seen Daniel Shiffman use. It's not at all difficult to implement nor even that complicated.

The first thing I did was to make it interactive by adding a repulsion from the mouse position. This made it a little more fun to play with. I added a couple extra features to allow the boids to push away from the mouse at greater than their typical max velocity and acceleration. 

After doing that, I sort of merged it with the bear prototype, and I replaced the boids with the squirrel asset. to make it look better I had the squirrel stay oriented horizontally and just flip according to its direction of movement. It was interesting. 

I added in the Player class from my prior prototoype in order to enable control of the bear by the controller instead of by the mouse. Much better.

I replaced the squirrel with a bee asset I purchased from Etsy. Fun. the bees go back to rotating like boids rather than the flipping like the squirrel


11/30/25

After Kenji played the prototype, he suggested making the bees spawn from a beehive instead of just the mouse position (he had played around with making as many boids as he could). I added a beehive asset (again purchased from Etsy) and had the bees spawn from there on mouse click. They still evade the bear. 

I also added a feature where the bees despawn if they happen to come close to the beehive. They don't seek the hive, which perhaps they should.
